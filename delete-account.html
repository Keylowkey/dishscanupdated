Delete Your Capture & Cook Account

This page explains how to delete your account in the Capture & Cook app, published by Capture & Cook LLC, and exactly what data is deleted and kept.

Delete it yourself, in the app

Deletion is immediate and permanent. You do not need to contact us.

  1. Open Capture & Cook and sign in to the account you want to delete.
  2. Tap your profile avatar in the top-right corner of the screen.
  3. In the menu that opens, scroll to the bottom and tap Delete account, shown in red.
  4. Confirm when asked. Your account is deleted right away and you are signed out.

If you cannot open the app

Email support@captureandcook.com from the address on the account, with the subject Delete my account. We verify the request and delete the account within 30 days.

What is deleted

All of the following are erased permanently and cannot be recovered:

DataWhat it covers
Account and sign-inYour email address, password, and account identifier
ProfileName, username, avatar, allergy and dietary preferences, saved address or zip code
Nutrition and healthEvery logged meal and your daily calorie goal
RecipesYour cooking history, favorites, and scanned dishes
Community activityReactions, views, follows and followers, blocked-user list, and recipes you sent or received
DevicesPush notification tokens, so notifications stop reaching your phone
PhotosDish photos stored on your profile and in your history

What is kept, and for how long

  • Posts, comments, and replies you made in the community. These stay so that conversations other people took part in are not broken. Your name is removed from them and replaced with [deleted user], and they can no longer be traced back to you. If you want a specific post removed, delete it in the app before you delete your account.
  • Content moderation records. If a post of yours was reported, the report is retained for up to 12 months so that repeat abuse can be acted on. It holds no personal information about you.
  • Records we are required to keep. Where the law requires it, or where a record is needed to resolve a dispute or investigate fraud or abuse, we may retain limited information for as long as that obligation lasts, and no longer.

Everything else is removed from our live systems immediately. Encrypted backups are cycled out within 30 days.

Deleting the app is not the same thing

Uninstalling Capture & Cook removes it from your phone but leaves your account on our servers. Use the steps above to delete the account itself.
